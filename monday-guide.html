02💡

What is monday.com?

monday.com is a cloud-based work platform. Teams plan, track and discuss work on colourful visual boards instead of scattered emails, spreadsheets and chats. It works in your web browser and on a phone app.

  • ✅Task lists
  • 📊Spreadsheets
  • ✉️Email threads
  • 📁File folders
  • 📅Calendars
  • 📈Reports
➜

One shared board

Every task has an owner, a status, a due date, its files and its conversation, all in one row that everyone can see.

The monday.com product family

🗂️

Work Management

Projects, tasks and team workflows. This is what most of our boards use.

🤝

CRM

Contacts, relationships and pipelines, such as donors, partners or leads.

🎫

Service

Tickets and requests: raise an issue, route it, track it until it is resolved.

🛠️

Dev

Sprints, bugs and roadmaps for software and product teams.

✨

The AI layer

monday.com also includes AI tools that work across its products. In our workspace you may see buttons such as Sidekick, Agents and Vibe.

🧠 AI assistant (Sidekick)🤖 AI agents🧩 App builder (Vibe)

ℹ️ Which AI features you can use depends on our plan and available AI credits.

❤️ Why we use it at Amitabha Foundation

  • One place for projects. No more hunting through inboxes and chat groups.
  • Clear ownership. Every task has a named person in charge.
  • Less email. Conversations stay on the task they belong to.
  • Visible progress. Anyone can see what is done, stuck or waiting.
03🧬

How monday.com is organised

Think of it like nested boxes. Each level sits inside the one above it. Learn these seven words and the rest of monday.com makes sense.

  1. 🏢

    Account

    Our organisation's whole monday.com space, with its own web address.

    Ours: amitabhafoundation.monday.com

  2. 🗄️

    Workspace

    A big area for a team or department that holds its boards, docs and dashboards.

    Example: our team workspace

  3. 📂

    Folder

    An optional way to group related boards inside a workspace.

    Example: a folder for HR boards

  4. 📋

    Board

    A table for one project or process. This is where the work happens.

    Example: Recruitment Pipeline

    🧱 Columns: the fields every item has, e.g. Status, Owner, Date
  5. 🎨

    Group

    A coloured section of a board, such as a stage, a month or a team.

    Example: New applicants

  6. 📝

    Item

    One row: one task, one candidate, one request.

    Example: one job candidate

    💬 Updates: the item's comments, files and history
  7. ↳

    Subitem

    A smaller step tucked under an item, like a checklist line.

    Example: Schedule interview

Getting started in 5 minutes

Do these six steps once, in order. After that, you are ready for the tutorials.

  1. ✉️

    Log in

    Accept the email invite, or sign in at amitabhafoundation.monday.com (opens in a new tab).

  2. 🙂

    Set up your profile

    Click your picture (top right): add a photo, your full name and your time zone.

  3. 🧭

    Tour the left sidebar

    Home My Work Workspaces Favourites Search

  4. 🔔

    Tour the top bar

    Notifications bell, updates inbox, search, Invite and your profile.

  5. 📋

    Open your first board

    Pick a workspace, open a board, and star ⭐ it as a favourite.

  6. 📱

    Get the mobile app

    Install monday.com from the App Store or Google Play and allow notifications.

ℹ️
Notemonday.com updates its design from time to time. If a button looks slightly different, look for the same label or icon nearby.

Goal: make a new board for a project or process.

  1. In the left sidebar, open the workspace where the board should live.
  2. Click + Add new (or the + next to the workspace name) and choose Board.
  3. Type a clear board name, e.g. "Volunteer Day 2026".
  4. Choose who can see it (main, private or shareable) and what each item is called (Task, Candidate, Request…).
  5. Click Create board.
  6. From a template instead: choose Templates / Choose from templates, preview one, then click Use template.

  7. Archive or delete: ⋯ → Archive or Delete.
  8. Restore: click your profile picture → Archive (or Trash), find the item, click Restore.

Goal: find exactly the items you need on a busy board.

  3. Filter: click Filter and choose conditions, e.g. Status is "Stuck".

Goal: let monday.com do repeat jobs for you. Each automation is a recipe: a trigger (When…) plus an action (Then…).

⚡ WhenStatus changes to Done

✅ ThenNotify the manager
  1. Open the board and click Automate (top right of the board).
  2. Browse the ready-made recipes, or choose to create a custom automation.
  3. Click each underlined part of the sentence to fill it in, e.g. which status, which person.
  4. Click Create automation. It now runs on its own.
  5. To pause or edit it later, go back to Automate and find it in the board's automation list.

5 beginner recipes

  • When status changes to Done, then notify the manager.
  • When the due date arrives, then notify the assignee.
  • When an item is created, then assign a person.
  • When status changes, then move the item to a group.
  • Every week, then create a recurring item.

09⚖️

Do's and don'ts

A quick side-by-side check before you start working on a shared board.

✅ Do

  • Give every task one owner (PIC) and a due date.
  • Write updates and decisions on the item itself.
  • @mention the person who needs to act.
  • Keep the Work Status up to date as work moves on.
  • Use the Create New Task view on our workflow board.
  • Attach quotations, invoices and photos to the item.
  • Archive finished work to keep boards tidy.
  • Ask the board owner before changing a shared board's columns.

⛔ Don't

  • Discuss tasks only in chat apps or email.
  • Leave a status that's no longer true.
  • Create duplicate items for the same task.
  • Delete items you could archive.
  • Put a shared email in Taskee before choosing the PIC.
  • Set Work Status to Assigned before the details are complete.
  • Invite outside guests to boards with private data.
  • Paste passwords or sensitive personal data into updates.
